## Decided (summary)

- Learning has three tiles: **Discover**, **Review**, **Mix**, with icons and labels (Discover the sprout, Review the circle arrow, Mix a new icon). Tapping one opens **Start** (10 minutes, skill Mix) and **Options…** (Skill: Mix · Reading · Listening · Words · Grammar; Length: 5 · 10 · 20 · 30; Review also "Harder questions only").
- Master is no longer a mode of its own: its reversed questions come inside Review for well-known words. Defaults used: an exercise card's banner shows Review for them (with the minutes chip), and the session line names the tile ("10-minute Review").
- A **Resume** bar above the tiles while a session is going.
- Topic sessions start from each topic in the libraries ("Practise this topic").
- Replaced: Practise for X min / Mixed session (B-12), By skill and its Short/Medium/Long known-share bands (PD-04), Just one mode, the typed 3–60 length.
- B-10, B-11: built 2026-10-29 (common library import; Add a text's third way).
