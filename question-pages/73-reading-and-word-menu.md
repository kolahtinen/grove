# 73 — Reading page and word menu: open points

The owner's-test group "reading page and word menu" (O-12, O-14, O-15, O-16, O-17, O-19, O-21, O-22; decided on question-pages/64) written into requirements. Two points were still open, both from O-19 (no To read list for Swedish).

## 1. Swedish loses "To read". What happens to words already marked To read? (default: they become To remember)

- **Become To remember**: every To read mark turns into To remember, keeping its schedule.
- Become unmarked leaves: To read marks are dropped; the learner marks them again if wanted.

**Answer:** Become To remember (the default).

## 2. With only To remember left, every Review question would play its sentence aloud and hide it until answered. Is that right? (default: yes, shown when the device has no Swedish voice)

- Spoken and hidden: as To remember works today; shown if there's no Swedish voice.
- Alternate shown and spoken: each review switches between reading and listening.
- **Shown, with Play**: sentence shown, with a button to hear it.

**Answer:** Shown, with Play.
