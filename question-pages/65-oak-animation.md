## Decided

Eight frames, about 16 s: (1) wide oak, läsa the only amber leaf; (2) zoom in, it shakes; (3) the stem snaps; (4) zoom out, it drifts down swaying; (5) zoom in to leaf-covered ground; (6) it lands among the others; (7) zoom out, the oak grows a little; (8) the ground and other details fade, leaving the oak alone, which is the logo. Captions: 1–2 "Every word you save is a leaf.", 3–6 "When you know it, it falls to the ground.", 7 "Your ground feeds everything you read next."; frame 8 has none. Flat, minimal, shaded with a warm light side and a cool shadow side. The oak becomes grove's logo everywhere (RQ-310, RQ-311 and Appendix A.5 change in an addendum; N-34 unchanged). Defaults: both looks, Skip stays, reduced motion shows four stills (frames 1, 4, 6, 8) with their captions.
