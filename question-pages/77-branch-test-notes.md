## Decided (summary)

- Terms: agreed at sign-up, on each new device, and again when the terms change (stored version, question-pages/76, 16). Changes constitution 3.1 and G-01 FR-011.
- Level: at sign-up the learner picks a level (A1…C1, with descriptions) or takes the starting check; either can be redone from Settings.
- Home: one page with three headed groups. Learning: Practise for X min, By skill, Just one mode (Resume first while a session goes). Library: Common library (its texts by topic with the level filter, and the common dictionary: grove's whole word list) and Your library (your texts, Add a text, your dictionary, your leaves). Progress: Progress. Tiles with labels (question-pages/76, 2).
- Practise with no unread text: before the session, offer Pick from the common library (your level first), Add a text, Practise without a text.
- Add a text: one page with the two ways side by side, each with an explainer, each leading to its own page.
- Texts from your AI: one step per screen under an always-visible step list (✓ done, ● now, ○ next), one main button and Back, a confirmation after each action; the tactic choice out of sight unless answers keep failing.
