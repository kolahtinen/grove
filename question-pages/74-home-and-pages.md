## Summary of the new Home

- **Home** is a grid of square tiles, each showing only an icon; tapping one opens it in place across the grid (title, explanation, Enter), pushing the rest down. Tiles: **Resume** (only while a session is going), **Practise for X min**, **Texts** (topics, new texts from the AI or the learner's own, the library's texts per topic), **By skill** (reading, listening, words and phrases, grammar; short, medium, long: long has the highest share of known words), **Dictionary**, **Just one mode** (Discover, Review or Master alone), **Progress** (level, minutes, ground). The logo goes Home (O-02).
- **☰ menu** in the header: Account, Settings, Help (a bubble, O-05), Sign out.
- **A session** (by time, topic or skill) is planned by grove from items with known durations: a mix of Discover, Review and Master, in grove's order, one exercise at a time, with "10-minute session · 3 of 8 · about 6 min left" and a progress bar.
- **Prompting** (in Texts): two dropdowns, AI and version, then a card with the wizard whose steps are the prompting phases (copy prompt, paste answer).
- **Settings**: speech speed and the voice help, Speak words, the starting level.
- **Colours**: palette 14 and the mode banners (question 15).
- **Terms** (O-03, question-pages/64): one section per screen, Next unlocks after about 3 s, one Agree at the end confirming 13+ and the terms.
