Terms of use and privacy notice

Draft for the owner to review. Last changed 24 October 2026.

Who grove is for

Grove is a language-learning app run by one person (the owner) on their own computer in Finland. Right now it is open only to the owner and a few invited course reviewers. You must be 13 or over to use grove.

The reviewer account is shared: every reviewer signs in to the same account, so reviewers see and can change each other’s activity on it.

Texts you share

Only share texts you have the right to share. If a text breaks someone’s copyright, the first time leads to a fixed suspension and the second time to a ban.

Texts you get from your own AI chat or add yourself get only automatic checks, so they may contain mistakes.

What grove keeps and why

  • Your email address: to sign you in and to send password-reset emails.
  • Your password: kept only by Firebase, scrambled (hashed); grove never stores it.
  • Your learning history and progress (in later versions): so grove can give you material at your level.
  • Each sign-in’s agreement to these terms, with its time: to show you agreed.
  • A security log of sign-ins, failed sign-ins, lockouts and password-reset requests, with the internet address used: to protect accounts. It is deleted after 30 days.

Grove keeps nothing else and loads nothing from other websites while you use it.

Who else handles your data

Firebase Authentication (Google) handles sign-in and passwords for grove. Cloudflare carries the connection between your browser and grove. What you paste into your own AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another) is governed by that AI’s own terms, not grove’s.

Your rights

To get a copy of your data, or to have it deleted, ask the owner. There is no button for this yet.
