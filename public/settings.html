Speech

How well the speed works depends on your device's voice.

No Swedish voice?

This device has no Swedish voice, so grove stays silent rather than read Swedish with another language's voice.

To add one:

  • iPhone, iPad, Mac: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Swedish.
  • Android: Settings → Text-to-speech → your speech engine's language data → Swedish.
  • Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech → add Swedish.
  • On a computer, Chrome and Microsoft Edge often have Swedish voices while online.

Then reload grove.
