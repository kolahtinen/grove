Two ways to get Swedish texts into your library.

Texts from your AI Beta

grove gives you prompts to paste into a free AI chat (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or another). You paste its answer back; grove checks it and adds texts at your level, mostly in words you know. About 5 minutes.

Your own text Beta
