How grove works

grove helps you learn Swedish by reading and listening to texts made almost entirely of words you already know. A few new words appear in each text, so you understand what you read without translating, and the new words stick because you meet them in a story.

grove's words

grove
The app. Its motto: grow from what you know.
Leaf
A word, phrase or grammar pattern you've saved but haven't learned yet. Tap a word while reading to save it as a leaf.
Leaves
The one place that holds all your leaves. Open it from Your library.
To read
A mark on a leaf for languages with their own writing system (such as Chinese): you want to recognise it when reading. Not used for Swedish; earlier To read marks became To remember.
To remember
A mark on a leaf: you want to be able to recall and use it; drilled leaves are reviewed in sentences. The only mark for Swedish.
Ground
Everything you've learned: the words, phrases and patterns that have fallen from your leaves, plus the starting words for your level. New texts are written mostly from your ground, and your level is worked out from it.
Fall
A leaf joining your ground. It happens by itself when you've answered a leaf right with at least 4 days between reviews, or when you choose to.
Let it fall
A button on any leaf: answer its meaning once, and if you're right, it falls to the ground straight away.
Pick up
Taking a word back from the ground to drill it again, if you find you've forgotten it.
Drill
Practising your leaves with short reviews spaced further and further apart, until they fall.
Setup
Each package starts in a new chat with your AI: a few setup prompts give it the rules, the answer format and your known words, and it just replies "OK". After that, short prompts in the same chat ask for the next texts. If your AI forgets, tap I'm in a new chat to send the setup again.
Reading text
A text written for reading. Each part has reading texts and a listening text.
Listening text
A text written to be heard, in short spoken sentences. It opens in listening mode: you hear it first, then see it.
Session
A planned run of practice for the time you choose (5, 10, 20 or 30 minutes), from Discover, Review or Mix, or on one topic, one exercise at a time.
Discover / Review / Mix
The three Learning tiles on Home. Discover gives you new texts, Review the words you are learning, Mix both. Each has Start (10 minutes, all skills) and Options… (reading, listening, words or grammar, and the length).
Harder question
A question the other way round, from English to Swedish, for a word you already know well. Harder questions come in Review; Review's Options… can give you only those.
Fill bars
When a session hasn't enough new texts, bars for listening and reading show how many minutes of texts it still needs. They fill as you add texts and turn into Start your session.
Exercise
One item of a session: a text to read or hear, a word to review, a harder question, or a practice review.
Practice review
A review of a leaf that isn't due yet, used to fill a session's time. It's extra practice: it never changes when the leaf is due next.
Your library
Your own texts by topic, Add a text, your dictionary and your leaves.
Common library
grove's own texts (grove texts) by topic, each with its level, and the common dictionary.
Common dictionary
grove's whole Swedish word list: search any word for its English meaning and level, and add it as a leaf.
grove text
A text grove provides in the common library, approved by grove's owner. Added to your library, it is marked "grove text".
Your dictionary
Every word you've met in your own texts.
Meaning check
Every word gets a meaning at once, from grove's word list (or from you, for a word the list doesn't have). Your AI then checks it in the sentence where you met it.

Discover and Review

Discover
Read and listen to new texts. Each one is mostly your ground, with a few new words.
Review
Drill the leaves that are due. A leaf is due when you're about to forget it; fading leaves come first.
Harder questions (was Master)
For leaves you already know well (2 or more days between reviews): you're asked the other way round, from English to Swedish. They are part of Review.
Marking
After a text, grove shows its new words and you choose for each: To remember, or not (then it goes straight to your ground).

Levels and new words

CEFR
The Common European Framework of Reference: six levels from A1 (beginner) through A2, B1, B2 and C1 to C2 (near-native). grove estimates yours from your ground and the Swedish Kelly word list.
Known words
grove asks the AI to write texts where about 95 out of every 100 words are from your ground. That's the share at which most people follow a text and pick up the new words from it.
Your own AI
grove never calls an AI itself. It gives you a prompt to paste into a free AI chat, and checks the answer you paste back.
