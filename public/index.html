Welcome to grove

Every new word you meet is a leaf. Mark the ones you don't know yet and grove drills them, in sentences you have read, until you know them; then they fall to the ground.

Your ground is everything you know. New texts are written mostly from it, so they stay readable as they get harder.

Practice comes in three stages: Discover new texts, Review your leaves when they're due, and Master your strongest ones.
