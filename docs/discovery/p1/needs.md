## Four worlds

- **Subject** (what the app holds information about): words and grammar from open dictionaries and word lists, whose authors need their licences kept (N-p1-32).
- **Usage** (users, owners): learners, students, teachers, the owner; children under 13 and their parents (N-p1-33).
- **System** (hosting, outside services, their operators): free hosting that sleeps and free databases that pause (see risks); learners' own AI chats (free tiers have message limits); browser voices.
- **Development** (me): one person running and checking everything (N-p1-34).
