# Grove's own terms

The words grove uses for its ideas, in the app and in every spec from G-02 on. The help page (RQ-370) is built from this list. Decided on question-pages/56; earlier names in brackets are the prototypes' and must not appear in the app.

| Term | Meaning | Rules (requirements) |
|---|---|---|
| **grove** | The app. Tagline: **grow from what you know**. | RQ-309 |
| **Leaf** | A saved word, phrase or grammar pattern that isn't learned yet. [p0: Heard words, Reading list; p1: drilled word] | question-pages/56 |
| **Leaves** | The one collection of all your leaves. | question-pages/56 |
| **To remember** | A mark on a leaf: you want to be able to recall and use it. Replaces p0's 'Heard words'. A leaf can have both marks. | question-pages/56 |
| **To read** | A mark on a leaf: you want to recognise it when reading. Replaces p0's 'Reading list'. | question-pages/56 |
| **Ground** | Everything you've learned: the words, phrases and patterns that have fallen from your leaves, and the starting words for your level. New texts are written mostly from your ground; your level is estimated from it. | RQ-03, RQ-34, RQ-113 |
| **Fall** | A leaf joining the ground: automatically when its review gap reaches 4 days after a right answer, by choice at the retire check, or with **Let it fall**. [p0: retire] | RQ-15, RQ-16, RQ-60 |
| **Let it fall** | A button on any leaf: answer its meaning once (a grammar pattern: confirm), and if right, it falls. | question-pages/56 |
| **Pick up** | Taking a word back from the ground to drill it again. | RQ-16 |
| **Drill** | Practising leaves with spaced reviews until they fall. | RQ-60 and G-04 |
