# Grove's own terms

The words grove uses for its ideas, in the app and in every spec from G-02 on. The help page (RQ-370) is built from this list. Decided on question-pages/56; earlier names in brackets are the prototypes' and must not appear in the app.

| Term | Meaning | Rules (requirements) |
|---|---|---|
| **grove** | The app. Tagline: **grow from what you know**. | RQ-309 |
| **Leaf** | A saved word, phrase or grammar pattern that isn't learned yet. [p0: Heard words, Reading list; p1: drilled word] | question-pages/56 |
| **Leaves** | The one collection of all your leaves. | question-pages/56 |
| **To remember** | A mark on a leaf: you want to be able to recall and use it; drilled leaves are reviewed in sentences. Replaces p0's 'Heard words'. The only mark for Swedish since addendum 003. | question-pages/56, 73 |
| **To read** | A mark on a leaf for languages with their own writing system (such as Chinese): you want to recognise it when reading. Not used for Swedish since addendum 003 (O-19); earlier To read marks became To remember. | question-pages/56, 64 (10), 73 |
| **Ground** | Everything you've learned: the words, phrases and patterns that have fallen from your leaves, and the starting words for your level. New texts are written mostly from your ground; your level is estimated from it. | RQ-03, RQ-34, RQ-113 |
| **Fall** | A leaf joining the ground: automatically when its review gap reaches 4 days after a right answer, by choice at the retire check, or with **Let it fall**. [p0: retire] | RQ-15, RQ-16, RQ-60 |
| **Let it fall** | A button on any leaf: answer its meaning once (a grammar pattern: confirm), and if right, it falls. | question-pages/56 |
| **Pick up** | Taking a word back from the ground to drill it again. | RQ-16 |
| **Drill** | Practising leaves with spaced reviews until they fall. | RQ-60 and G-04 |
| **Setup** | The first prompts of a package, sent in a new chat: the rules, the answer format, the summary and your known words; your AI only replies "OK". Later prompts in the same chat just ask for the next texts. | RQ-390, RQ-391 (addendum 002) |
| **Reading text** / **Listening text** | The two kinds of text in a part: a reading text is for reading; a listening text is written to be heard and opens in listening mode. | RQ-393 (addendum 002) |
| **Meaning check** | grove gives every word a meaning at once, from its word list or from you; your AI then checks it in the sentence where you met it. | RQ-396, RQ-398 (addendum 002) |
| **Session** | A planned run of exercises of a chosen length (Practise for X min, By skill, a topic, or Just one mode), one exercise at a time. | RQ-421, G-09 FR-904 |
| **Exercise** | One item of a session: a text to read or hear, a word to review, a Master question, or a practice review. | RQ-421, G-09 FR-905 |
| **Practice review** | A review of a leaf that isn't due yet, used to fill a session's time; its answer never changes the leaf's schedule. | RQ-421, G-09 FR-905; question-pages/76 (1, F1) |
| **Your library** | The learner's own texts by topic, Add a text, your dictionary and your leaves. | RQ-430, G-09 FR-915 |
| **Common library** | grove's shared texts by topic with their levels, and the common dictionary. | RQ-431, G-09 FR-916 |
| **Common dictionary** | grove's whole Swedish word list, searchable, with meanings and levels; a word can be added as a leaf. | RQ-432, G-09 FR-917 |
| **Your dictionary** | The words met in the learner's own texts (the Dictionary page). | G-03 FR-316, G-09 FR-915 |
| **grove text** | A text grove provides in the common library, approved by the owner and belonging to no learner; added to Your library, it is marked "grove text", never Beta. | RQ-438, G-09 FR-916; B-11 |
