## Missed-needs checklist

Checked across both prototypes: accounts, privacy and personal data (N-p1-50, 53, 57 sources), errors and failures (correctness: N-p1-39), offline and flexibility (N-p1-52), accessibility (withdrawn in p1 on page 27), onboarding (starting level; confidence in the method), data export and deletion (covered by personal data protection; as requirements PR-p1-245).

## Four worlds

- **Subject:** authors of open dictionaries and word lists (licences).
- **Usage:** learners, students, teachers (cut for now), the owner, parents of children under 13.
- **System:** the owner's computer and the relay tunnel; learners' own AI chats; browser voices.
- **Development:** one person (the owner).
