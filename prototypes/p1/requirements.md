# Prototype requirements: grove / p1

**Purpose:** the first grove prototype: a Swedish learner that grows a tree of known words, phrases and grammar patterns, with comprehensible-input reading and listening, and AI material by copy-paste.

The living requirements list for one throwaway prototype (phase 1). Each round adds or changes requirements here. A new requirement is checked against the existing ones; conflicts are raised before anything changes. Phase 2 combines the requirements of the prototypes that aren't rejected into `docs/requirements.md`.

- **Status:** active / changed / dropped / implicit: undecided / denied. Implicit requirements are things a build does that nobody asked for; I confirm (→ active), deny or leave them undecided.
- **Needs:** the need IDs behind it (`docs/discovery/p1/needs.md`), or "prototype detail, no need".
- **Feasibility:** its rating (F-…: easy / possible with limits / not feasible) and any reduction (X-…).
