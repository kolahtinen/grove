Sketch: Home

  • For: what Home is for and how it makes sense to a learner, the first time and after a week.
  • Screens: Home → what Discover does → Your library → Progress → the ☰ menu (and Resume, from Home).
  • Open: what Home shows first: A the next step, B where you stand today, C a clear map of everything (your pick on question-pages/91, 3, with "show me all three").
  • States: Normal is a learner after a week (A2, 12 words due, a session going). Empty is a brand-new learner: no level, no texts, nothing due.
B-04 "There should be a clearer divide on the home page." Today: three headings over look-alike tiles; Library and Progress rows half empty.
B-12…B-14 Learning is Discover, Review and Mix; each gives Start (10 min) and Options…. Resume is a bar above them while a session is going.
B-17 Going back: "‹ Home" at the top of each page, Back closes what is open first (question-pages/80).
Questions this sketch settles, and what is odd on Home today
  • What Home shows first, and how a learner knows what to do next (FR-901, FR-904).
  • What tapping a tile does: a panel, a sheet, or straight to its page (FR-901, FR-903).
  • What a brand-new learner sees before they have a level or any texts (FR-901, G-06 FR-605).
  • Names and one-line explanations for the tiles (FR-901, FR-915, FR-916).

Seen on the build today (dev server, 390 px and 1280 px, 2026-10-31):

  1. Every tile looks the same and says one word; Home tells you nothing about today (nothing due, no minutes, no level).
  2. Two taps to start anything: tile, then Start in its panel. Library and Progress open a panel with just one button.
  3. A new learner can't reach Home: after the welcome they are sent to the starting level, and its "‹ Home" link sends them back to it.
  4. A new learner's Discover › Start opens a session page that only says "Not enough new texts"; Home then shows "Resume: 10-minute Discover" for a session that never began.
  5. Review with nothing due says "Add a text first" and offers Go to Discover, which runs into the same wall.
  6. Discover's line still says "New texts to read and hear"; it now also has Words and Grammar.
  7. On a computer the tiles stay in a narrow column, each tile 170 px across, with Progress alone in its row.

Example data: made up for this sketch (learner "Maja", A2). Nothing you tap is saved.
