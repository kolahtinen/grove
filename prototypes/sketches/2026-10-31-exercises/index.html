The same small fixes in every version (from playing the build at 390 px): each exercise names its skill and says what to do in one line; one count for the session; right and wrong look the same everywhere; Next sits in one place and comes when the exercise is done; a text in a session has no links that leave it; listening starts with Play.

What I saw in the build (2026-10-31, the demo learner, 390 px)
  1. The banner says only Discover or Review: a Words, Grammar, reading or listening exercise all say "Discover", and nothing says what to do (Review: no line like "pick the meaning").
  2. Two counts compete: "1 of 3" for the session and "1 of 4" for the word cards; Grammar's "1 · Learn / 2 · Spot it" are tabs you can skip with.
  3. Right/wrong differs: Review marks ✓ ✗ and says "Not quite — the right answer is marked."; the phrase spotlight says "Not quite." and its locked choices fade to half strength, so the right one is hard to read.
  4. The word asked about in Review isn't marked in its sentence, and the English never shows after answering.
  5. A text opens on the text page with "Done — next exercise" at the very top (you can skip it unread) while the page ends with "Back to Home", "‹ Your library" and Practise, which all leave the session.
  6. A listening text opens as a blurred reading page; you have to find Listen, then scroll past the whole blurred text to reach Play. Its card in the session says "Read it, answer its questions".
  7. Read again and listen again look exactly like a new text and ask for a rating again.
  8. Spot it: punctuation sits apart from words ("sex ."); dashed outlines run into the next word.
  9. After the last word card the dots overlap Next.
  10. The session bar is the browser's default blue, not grove's green; "about 0 min left" on a 1-question Review; the summary lists "0 texts read", "0 min of practice" and still offers "Leave for now".
  11. Review's chip says "1 min" for a 20-second question.
