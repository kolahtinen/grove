Found in today's build (walked at 390 px, 2026-10-31):

  1. Texts from your AI: pasting an answer grove can't read leaves "Reading the answer…" on screen for good (a script error); no fix-it prompt appears, and the next paste says "This part is already added", which is wrong.
  2. Coming back to the wizard after that shows "Fix-it prompt (part 1 of 3)" with no word of what failed, and the step list has lost "Send the setup".
  3. The step list grows a step (Send the setup) only after Get texts, so the steps you see first aren't the real ones.
  4. "Package: 0 of 3 parts in", "Version", the three ways of asking: words the learner has to guess at. The AI and Version menus are squeezed onto one line.
  5. From the common library opens a page whose link says "‹ Home", so there's no way back to Add a text. The level filter says A1 for an A2 learner.
  6. Tapping a library title leaves the list for the reading page (B-16 still open); after + Add, only a line at the top of the list says so.
  7. Your own text: Save text jumps straight to the reading page with no "Saved", shows "How much did you understand?" before the text can be read, and ends in "Back to Home" while the top link says "‹ Your library".
  8. The own text's AI check isn't explained as a step (what it is, how long, what comes after), and "waiting for your AI" in the list doesn't say what to do.
