Sketch: Dictionary pick up and let fall

B-28: “dict should have both 'pick up' and 'let fall'. these should be two sides of the same button, which is on the right hand side of the current div.”

Brief. Every word row in your dictionary and in the common dictionary gets one two-sided button at its right: a leaf can Let it fall, a word in your ground can be Picked up, and a word that's neither (common dictionary only) can be picked up as a new leaf.
Screens: Your library → Your dictionary → the meaning check that Let it fall asks → back with the row flipped; and the common dictionary search.
Open: how the two-sided button looks and acts (A, B, C below), and what you see after a tap.

Questions it settles:

  • What the two-sided button looks like and how it flips (the versions).
  • What a tap shows afterwards: a line in the row, a toast with Undo, or the switch itself.
  • Where Let it fall's meaning check sits when it starts from the dictionary.
  • Already decided (question-pages/85, 3): both dictionaries get it; ground → Pick up, leaf → Let it fall, neither → Pick up (adds a leaf).
