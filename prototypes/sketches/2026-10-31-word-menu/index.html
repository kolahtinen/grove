Sketch: Word menu

  • For: the sheet that opens when you tap a word in a text (G-03 FR-305).
  • Try it: tap any word in the text inside the phone, or use Word below to open the menu on a name, a ground word, a leaf, a word with two patterns or a word in a phrase.
  • Versions: Now is the menu as built today, for comparison. A, B and C are three different fixes; pick one at the bottom.
Your words "Just things that make the UI a bit hard to intuit. Give me a few sketches for the word menu alongside the current version and I can tell you what I want."
1 · Two Pick up buttons. A word with a grammar pattern shows a small Pick up under each pattern and a big Pick up for the word; nothing says which picks up what. (Try Bröden in Now.)
2 · Names show empty rows. Tapping a name shows "Dictionary form —" and "Meaning here —". (Try Lina in Now.)
Also seen in the built menu, and what this sketch settles
  • Esc doesn't close it; only Close and tapping outside do (A, B and C close on Esc).
  • A pattern's full explanation sits inline in the sheet, with its Pick up after the last word of it.
  • Four equal buttons: To remember, Explain, Report sentence and Pick up look the same, with no hint what each does.
  • The status says "In your ground — you know it" above the facts, while the big button at the bottom offers Pick up for the same word.
  • To remember on a ground word does what Pick up does (FR-409, FR-411: both make it a drilled leaf, due now), so a ground word has two ways to the same thing.
  • A leaf with no meaning stored shows "Meaning here" with nothing after it (seen on stan in the A1-02 text; the Empty state below).
  • A new word in a grove text has no Pick up at all, only To remember (Save as leaf is only for your own texts).

It settles: how the word's facts, its one action, its patterns and phrases, and the lesser actions are laid out; what a name shows; whether the menu closes on Esc; what happens after Pick up or Let it fall (FR-305, FR-306, FR-409…FR-411, FR-926, FR-927).

Example data: a made-up text and made-up learner states, written into this page; the grammar patterns are from grove's A1–A2 ladder. Nothing you tap is saved. Speech isn't played in the sketch.
