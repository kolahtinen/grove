Sketch: Listening sentences

  • For: getting at one sentence of a listening text (replay, loop) without the long list of rows.
  • Places: a listening text's own page (Listen card), and a listening exercise inside a session.
  • Open: how the one button and its selection look (A, B, C), and how a sentence names itself.
B-33 "i don't want the long list of individual sentences like it is. maybe instead a button that opens a selection. in the selection bubble, sentences have 1-3 of their first words as an identifier, in stead of numbers. maybe also the number there first."
Questions this sketch settles
  • Where the one button sits and what its selection looks like: a bottom sheet, a popover, or one chosen sentence with a chip bubble (G-05 FR-506, FR-507).
  • How a sentence is named: its number, its first words, or both.
  • How a looping sentence shows, and how you stop it.
  • Whether the sentence that is playing is marked in the selection.
  • Whether a session's listening exercise gets the button too (today it has no sentences at all).

Example data: a made-up A2 listening text of 12 sentences. Play and Replay don't make a sound here; the highlighting moves as if they did. Nothing you tap is saved.
